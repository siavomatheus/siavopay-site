Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 6, 2026

This policy explains what happens to your information when you visit siavopay.com or contact us. SiavoPay is in development and is not processing live payments.

Who we are

This site is run by Matheus Oliveira, founder of SiavoPay. Contact: matheus@siavopay.com.

What we collect

  • Contact form: your name, email address and message.
  • Email: anything you send us directly.
  • Technical data: our hosting provider may process your IP address and basic browser data to deliver the site and keep it secure.

The site does not use advertising or analytics cookies. Please do not send card details, passwords or other sensitive information through the form.

Why we use it

We use your details only to reply to you and to keep a record of the conversation. Under EU and UK law, our legal basis is our legitimate interest in answering you, or the steps you ask us to take before a possible agreement.

Who receives it

We do not sell your information. We share it only with the providers that help us run the site:

  • Web3Forms: delivers contact form messages to our inbox.
  • Spaceship (Spacemail): email hosting.
  • GitHub Pages: website hosting.

These providers may process data outside your country, including in the United States. We may also disclose information when the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep messages for up to 12 months after our last contact, unless we need them longer for a legal reason or an ongoing business relationship.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may ask to access, correct, delete or restrict your information, object to its use, or receive a copy. Email us to make a request. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

Children

This site is intended for businesses and adults. It is not directed at children.

Changes

We will post any update on this page and change the effective date.
